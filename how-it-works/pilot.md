A pilot is a 30-day trial account. We give pilots by invitation. A pilot has
higher limits than the Free plan, and it needs no card.

## What we decided

| Limit | Pilot (30 days) |
| --- | --- |
| Length | 30 days |
| Stored video | 10 hours |
| New uploads | 10 hours in total |
| Watched minutes | 20,000 |
| Highest quality | 1080p |
| Verified domains | 3 |
| Projects | 3 |
| Our logo on the player | Hidden |

- When the 30 days end, the account returns to its plan, usually Free.
- Our team sets up each pilot by hand. The 30 days count from the day the
  account was created. We set up each pilot on a new account.

## Why

- We run a small number of pilots with real customers.
- Each pilot has a usage limit and a goal we agree before it starts.
- Higher limits let a pilot test a larger workload than Free allows.

## What it means for you

- If you want a pilot, ask us. We do not offer pilots to everyone.
- Use the usage endpoint to see your pilot limits and what is left. See
  [Usage and limits](/usage-and-limits).
- When the 30 days end, the Free limits apply again. You cannot upload more
  while you are over a Free limit. See [Free plan limits](/how-it-works/free-plan-limits).

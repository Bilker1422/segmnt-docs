This is the product changelog. It lists the changes that a customer can see, or
that change the API. Newest first.

For what is live right now, see [Feature status](/status). For the endpoint list,
see the [API overview](/api-overview).

## 0.18.1 — 20 October 2026

- **No silent deletes.** When a paid plan stops, the account owner now gets a
  warning e-mail before any video is deleted. It says how many videos will go,
  the date, and how to keep them. Videos are deleted only 30 days after the
  plan stopped, and at least 7 days after the warning e-mail.
- **Webhooks.** `video.removed` is now also sent when you delete a video
  (`data.reason` is `deleted_by_customer`) and when a video is removed after a
  plan stops (`plan_ended`). See [Webhooks](/webhooks).

## 0.14.0 — 17 October 2026

- Internal error-reporting work, so our team finds and fixes a fault sooner. No
  customer-visible change.

## 0.13.1 — 17 October 2026

- **Encoding.** We can encode more videos at the same time, so a large upload
  finishes sooner. Encoding speed and quality are unchanged. No API change.

## 0.13.0 — 17 October 2026

- **Playback protection.** Extra protection against link sharing and automated
  playback now runs behind the scenes. A normal viewer sees no change: a copied
  link still returns `403`, and playlists and segments are never challenged. See
  [How playback protection works](/playback-protection).

## 0.12.1 — 17 October 2026

- **Dashboard.** Opening a ready video shows its picture at once while the player
  finishes loading, instead of a black frame.
- **Embed page.** A video on your website shows its picture before the player
  loads, so a visitor does not see a black frame.
- **Player.** The player is downloaded once and reused, so a second video starts
  sooner.

## 0.12.0 — 16 October 2026

- **Demo.** https://segmnt.cloud/demo now plays a real protected video. The demo
  link is short-lived and locked to our domain, so a copied demo link returns
  `403`.
- **API:** a new public route, `GET /v1/demo/playback`, returns one short-lived
  playback token for the demo video.
- **Dashboard.** The sign-in and sign-up card now links the Privacy Policy and
  the Terms of Service.
- **Website.** The plan pages no longer show a "Most chosen" badge, and the site
  no longer states a VAT charge. See the
  [Terms of Service](https://segmnt.cloud/terms).

## 0.11.1 — 16 October 2026

- **Security page.** We corrected one sentence on the Security page to match the
  reviewed wording. No other change.

## 0.11.0 — 16 October 2026

- **Trust pages.** Three new pages are live: Content review and moderation
  policy, Data retention and deletion, and Security. The docs [Legal](/legal)
  page links them.
- **Moderation notice.** Holding or removing a video now tells the account owner
  by e-mail, with the reason, and sends the `video.held` or `video.removed`
  webhook with `data.reason`. A legal matter is not notified, and the file is
  kept as evidence. See [Reports and video review](/reports-and-review).
- **API:** `video.removed` joins the video events. See [Webhooks](/webhooks).

## 0.10.0 — 16 October 2026

- **Trust pages.** The Privacy Policy, the Terms of Service and the Acceptable
  Use Policy are live, linked from the footer "Legal" area and from the docs
  [Legal](/legal) page.

## 0.9.3 — 16 October 2026

- **Website.** The training use-case page now states the same retention rule as
  the home and pricing pages. No API change.

## 0.9.2 — 16 October 2026

- **Plans.** The plan pages now state the live rules: Starter stops at **20,000**
  watched minutes, overage is on Pro and Agency only, and stored videos are
  deleted **30 days** after a paid plan stops. See
  [Plans and payment](/billing).
- **Free plan.** Phone verification is no longer listed for the free plan. It
  stays **Coming soon**. See [Phone verification](/phone-verification).

## 0.9.1 — 16 October 2026

- **Website.** Our pages now match the live product: 4K playback, phone
  verification at sign-up and the free-plan review hold are **Coming soon**, and
  encoding, webhooks, embed codes, signed playback, domain lock and review
  frames are **Available**. See [Feature status](/status).
- **Plans.** The Pro and Agency overage rate is published: **5 SAR per 1,000
  watched minutes**. See [Plans and payment](/billing).
- **Support.** support@segmnt.cloud is in the footer, and the docs say we are
  based in Saudi Arabia.

## 0.9.0 — 16 October 2026

- **Retention.** Deleting a video also deletes its review frames, and no frame is
  kept longer than **30 days** after the delete. Frames are kept only while the
  video is under an open review, an abuse report or a legal hold.
- **Retention.** If a paid plan stops, the account's stored videos are deleted
  **30 days** later. Billing is paused, so this does not affect anyone today.

## 0.8.0 — 16 October 2026

- **Player.** The video now fills the screen in fullscreen.
- **Player.** The player script loads while the page is still parsing, so the
  first play starts sooner.
- **Player.** The player bundle is cached, so a repeat visit does not download it
  again.
- **Dashboard.** **Add to your website** is now one flow in two steps: get a
  token for the video, then put the token in the embed code.

## 0.7.1 — 15 October 2026

- Docs: the WordPress guide links the versioned plugin download.
- Small dashboard and pricing-page fixes. No API change.

## 0.7.0 — 15 October 2026

- **WordPress plugin v1 (1.0.0).** Install the zip, save a playback key and the
  project id once, then add `[segmnt id="vid_..."]` to a page. See
  [Add segmnt to WordPress](/wordpress).
- **API: playback-only API key scope.** An API key now has a `scope`: `full` or
  `playback`. A `playback` key can only create playback tokens, so a key on a
  web server cannot upload, delete, read account data or spend money. The key
  list and create answers return the scope.
- **API:** the `preview-token` route (for the dashboard's first-run preview) now
  returns `thumbnailUrl`.

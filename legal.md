These pages explain what we do with your data and how you may use segmnt.

- [Privacy Policy](https://segmnt.cloud/privacy) — what we collect, why we use
  it, who we share it with, and your rights under the Saudi Personal Data
  Protection Law (PDPL).
- [Terms of Service](https://segmnt.cloud/terms) — the agreement between you and
  segmnt.
- [Acceptable Use Policy](https://segmnt.cloud/acceptable-use) — what you may
  not host or share on segmnt.

We keep the fuller explanations in the docs. Where a policy page summarises one,
the docs page has the detail:

- [Moderation and held videos](/moderation) — the policy behind the
  [Content review and moderation policy](https://segmnt.cloud/moderation-policy).
- [Reports and video review](/reports-and-review) — what happens when we hold or
  remove a video.
- [Usage and limits](/usage-and-limits) — what we store and count, beside the
  [Data retention and deletion](https://segmnt.cloud/retention) page.
- [Playback protection](/playback-protection) — the token and domain lock
  behind the [Security](https://segmnt.cloud/security) page.

For a privacy request (access, a readable copy, correction or deletion of your
data), write to [privacy@segmnt.cloud](mailto:privacy@segmnt.cloud). For
anything else, write to [support@segmnt.cloud](mailto:support@segmnt.cloud).

These pages explain what we do with your data and how you may use segmnt.

- [Privacy Policy](https://segmnt.cloud/privacy) — what we collect, why we use
  it, who we share it with, and your rights under the Saudi Personal Data
  Protection Law (PDPL).
- [Terms of Service](https://segmnt.cloud/terms) — the agreement between you and
  segmnt.
- [Acceptable Use Policy](https://segmnt.cloud/acceptable-use) — what you may
  not host or share on segmnt.

For a privacy request (access, a readable copy, correction or deletion of your
data), write to [privacy@segmnt.cloud](mailto:privacy@segmnt.cloud). For
anything else, write to [support@segmnt.cloud](mailto:support@segmnt.cloud).

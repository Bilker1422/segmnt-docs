This version of segmnt does not include DRM. DRM is the technology that stops a
viewer from downloading or replaying a video. We do not have it, and we do not
describe our protections as DRM.

## What we decided

- No DRM in this version.
- Our protections are signed playback links and the domain check.
  Read [Playback links](/how-it-works/playback-links) and
  [How playback protection works](/playback-protection).

## Why

- DRM is high cost. It was outside the scope of this first version.
- A viewer who can see a video can record it. Our controls do not stop that.
  We say so openly, so you can plan for it.

## What it means for you

- Do not promise your customers that their videos cannot be copied.
- If you need DRM, this version does not meet that need.
- Our protections make casual copying and link sharing harder. They do not stop
  someone who has access to the video.

The Free plan lets you test a real video integration with no card. It has
fixed limits. This page explains why, and what happens at each limit.

## What we decided

| Limit | Free |
| --- | --- |
| Stored video | 2 hours |
| New uploads | 2 hours a month |
| Watched minutes | 2,000 a month |
| Highest quality | 720p |
| Verified domains | 1 |
| Projects | 1 |

- When you reach the watched-minutes limit, playback stops until the next UTC
  month starts.
- Free does not charge overage. Paid plans are not on sale yet, so a limit
  stays in place until the next month.

## Why

- A plan is a set of limits. Each limit decides what the plan covers, and
  when a customer needs the next plan.
- Free is for testing and small trials. It is not built for a busy site.

## What it means for you

- Check the usage endpoint before you ship a launch. It shows your limits and
  what is left. See [Usage and limits](/usage-and-limits).
- Free stops playback at the watched-minutes limit. Plan for that.
- Pilot accounts have higher limits. See [Pilot](/how-it-works/pilot).
